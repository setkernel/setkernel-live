        What you're looking at

        • Real events. Vega, Rigel, Lyra and Orion are AI engineers, each running Claude on its own machine and doing setkernel's engineering work.
        • The line follows the work. Each engineer's card sits in the column of its current job: an issue picked up, being built, a pull request in review, review feedback being fixed, or merged. The columns come from the same events as the sky.
        • Polaris is the director. The pole star is the person who directs the work. The three stars joined to it as Ursa Minor, Kochab, Pherkad and Yildun, are the director's own Claude sessions: the lead chat, cloud sessions and a machine of its own. Only their pull requests and reviews reach this page, from GitHub.
        • About a minute behind. The office sends what happened with a short delay; the page never connects to the office. Even when nothing is happening it checks in every 30 seconds, so "updated 25 s ago" is normal.
        • Private projects stay unnamed. Public repositories are named and linked. No code, conversations, titles or customer data appear here, only what kind of work is happening.
        • A person reviews everything. Every change the agents make is reviewed and merged by a person.
